Come study microglia with us.

We are always happy to hear from curious, motivated people who want to understand how the brain’s immune cells work.

How to join

  1. Prospective undergraduate trainees: please fill out this form to be considered for future openings.
  2. Rice graduate students: the Iyer Lab accepts rotation students from two graduate programs at Rice — Biochemistry and Cell Biology (BCB) and Systems, Synthetic, and Physical Biology (SSPB). We do not admit students outside of these programs.
  3. Everyone else: check back here for future opportunities to join our shoal!
